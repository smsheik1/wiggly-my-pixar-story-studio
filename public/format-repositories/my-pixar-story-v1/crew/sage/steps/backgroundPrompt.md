# backgroundPrompt

Criteria: brief-fit, style, spatial-action, continuity.

- brief-fit: The prompt carries the approved brief's room, dressing, light and layout without adding or dropping story details, and keeps the room empty of people.
- style: The prompt reads as a stylized animated feature set, not generic 3D, photoreal or stock: chunky stylized shapes, warm motivated light, a muted palette with one saturated accent, the signature accent kept out of the room. Apply the prompt rules in `task.craft`.
- spatial-action: The layout leaves room for the scene's action.
- continuity: Details match the master plate, the brief and the other plates.
Material defects, not taste, in every background step: an empty, generic, catalog or undressed room; "plain" or "unmarked" props; "blank" or "calm" walls; a look that reads as generic 3D, photoreal or stock. "Empty of people" (no characters, people or silhouettes) is required for compositing, but an empty set is still a defect. A muted, restrained, neutral, ivory or oatmeal palette is never a failure on its own and never rescues an empty room. Quote the offending words and give a concrete story-dressing repair drawn from the answers.
