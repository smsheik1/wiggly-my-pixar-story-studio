# editPlan

Criteria: timeline, narration-preservation, mix, continuity, provenance.

- timeline: Clips are assembled in order. The shell checks 30 fps alignment.
- narration-preservation: The locked narration is untouched.
- mix: The mix keeps the narration clear and the score and effects beneath it.
- continuity: Cuts between clips hold together.
- provenance: Every clip and sound traces to an approved, locked deliverable.
