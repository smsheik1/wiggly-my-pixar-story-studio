# roster

Criteria: completeness, age-variants, references.

- completeness: Every person the locked script names, and everyone the shots will need, is in the roster, and no one the story doesn't need.
- age-variants: One look per distinct visible age. Nearby ages may share one look, even across 18; a birthday alone is not a reason for a new look. A shared look used in a minor scene is marked minor.
- references: Each photo reference is bound to the right person and interpretation choices are recorded. Never accept a claim that photos show a difference nobody inspected.

Not a defect: a roster with fewer looks than ages in the script.
