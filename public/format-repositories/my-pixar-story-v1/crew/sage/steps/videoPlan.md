# videoPlan

Criteria: coverage, timing, keyframe-grounding, motion, continuity.

- coverage: Every shot is covered by clips.
- timing: The shell checks 30 fps alignment. You judge whether each clip's action fits its length.
- keyframe-grounding: Each clip starts from its approved keyframe.
- motion: One camera move per clip, with physically anchored action.
- continuity: People, props and light carry across clips and cuts.
