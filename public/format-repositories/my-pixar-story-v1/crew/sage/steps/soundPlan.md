# soundPlan

Criteria: story-fit, piano-score, effect-timing, provenance.

- story-fit: Sound serves the story and never competes with the locked narration.
- piano-score: A 60-second solo acoustic piano score.
- effect-timing: Story-serving effects land at the right moments.
- provenance: Each sound traces to the script or the shot plan, and every omission gives a reason.
