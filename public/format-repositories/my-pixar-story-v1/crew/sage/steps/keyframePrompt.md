# keyframePrompt

Criteria: scene-fit, reference-grounding, staging, camera, continuity.

- scene-fit: The prompt serves its shot's action and the script's beat.
- reference-grounding: It binds the setting plate first, then the character sheets in exact order.
- staging: People sit where the locked shot plan puts them, and the layout fits the plate.
- camera: The camera matches the locked shot's camera.
- continuity: People, props and light match the plates, the sheets and the neighboring keyframes.
