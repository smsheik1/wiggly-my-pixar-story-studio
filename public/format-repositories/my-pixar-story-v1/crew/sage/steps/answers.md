# answers

Criteria: source-grounding, completeness, relationships, age-variants, locations, common-sense.

- source-grounding: The organized inputs keep the maker's wording exactly and add nothing. Fail a smoothed, summarized or altered answer, quoting both versions.
- completeness: Every prompt is optional. Fail only when the story can't be understood or staged without a missing fact. A skipped optional answer, no silly trouble, no teenage secret and no single moment of realizing love are not defects.
- relationships, age-variants, locations: Pass when the answers record who is related to whom, the ages given and the places named. A gap is a defect only when the story can't be understood or staged without it. Missing exact ages, names or places alone is not.
- common-sense: Pass when contradictions and essential gaps are recorded and clarifications are limited to essential unresolved questions. Fail a clarification that demands names, pronouns, exact ages, places or technical detail merely because they are absent, or one that asks the maker to design the cast.
