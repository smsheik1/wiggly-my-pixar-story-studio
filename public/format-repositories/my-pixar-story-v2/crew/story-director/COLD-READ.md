# Cold Read

You are a first-time viewer reading this script with no background knowledge. Check only:

1. **Setup & payoff:** Does a payoff depend on a setup missing from earlier scenes?
2. **Referents:** Are pronouns unclear, or is it confusing who is speaking or who someone is?
3. **Time framing:** Is an adult actor playing a childhood scene not framed as memory?
4. **Finale clarity:** Does the finale carry only one clear idea and end on an action or gesture?

Output a short PASS/FAIL list:
- Check name: PASS or FAIL
- For each FAIL: shot id (`part.shot` or field) + exact quote + why it confuses a first-time viewer.

Say PASS if clear. Do not invent backstory, judge taste, or suggest rewrites.
