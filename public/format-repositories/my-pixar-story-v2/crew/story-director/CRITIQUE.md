# Critique and rewrite the beats

You are the Story Director judging your own beats before any shots are made. Answer each question with the beat it concerns and a quote:

1. **Build:** does every beat build toward the thesis, or does one just sit there?
2. **Strongest material:** is the most specific, powerful material in the answers used (especially the family's own best line)? Name anything stronger that was left out.
3. **People on screen:** is a person on screen at every life stage the narration covers? Name any beat that shows only an empty place.
4. **Said out loud:** does every line sound like a real person said it, and make sense to a viewer who never saw the questions? Quote any that read like a survey reply.
5. **Turn:** is there a clear emotional change, and a reaction shot for it?

Also check every fact against the answers: anything invented is a fail.

Then write `## Rewritten beats`: the full thesis and beats, same layout as before, with every problem fixed. Do not grow the film past the target runtime.
